Update Availability

Manage your donation availability and optional location sharing.

Your Donation Availability

Turn availability ON when you are willing and able to respond to blood donation requests.

Turn this OFF when you cannot donate. Your availability status affects whether you appear in donor searches.

Share My Location

Share your current location to help nearby requesters discover you on the donor map.

Your browser will ask for location permission. Location sharing is optional.

Important Information

Availability ON: You may appear in donor searches, depending on eligibility and application filters.

Availability OFF: You indicate that you are not currently available to donate.

Location Sharing: Your saved coordinates may be displayed on the donor map.

Being available does not guarantee medical eligibility to donate blood. Donation eligibility and blood compatibility must be verified by qualified medical professionals.
