ABOUT RAKTASETU

A Connection That Can Make a Difference

RaktaSetu is a blood donation and emergency donor finder platform built to connect people who need blood with voluntary donors.

Donors can manage their availability, while requesters can search for donors, submit blood requests and track their request status.
