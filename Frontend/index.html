ABOUT RAKTASETU

Making Blood Donor Search Easier

RaktaSetu is a web-based blood donation and emergency donor finder system. It provides a platform where donors can register their details and people can search for potential donors according to blood group and location.

The system is designed to make the process of finding potential blood donors more organized during emergency situations.
